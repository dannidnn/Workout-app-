📐 Why These Numbers

At 209 lbs sedentary, your TDEE is ~2,650 kcal. A 250–300 kcal deficit puts you at ~2,400 — deliberately moderate for a beginner. Cutting harder kills muscle and wrecks gym performance before you even get started. Target: 0.5 lb fat loss per week while building muscle. Rest days drop to 2,100 kcal. Keep protein at 180g every single day regardless.

PHASE-BY-PHASE NUTRITION ADJUSTMENTS

📈 Phase 1–2 (Weeks 1–8)

Stick to 2,400 kcal training / 2,100 rest. Protein 180g. This is your foundation. Don't overthink it — hit these numbers daily and fat loss happens automatically.

📈 Phase 3–4 (Weeks 9–16)

Drop training calories to 2,300 and rest to 2,000. By now your metabolism has adapted and you're stronger. A slightly tighter deficit keeps fat loss moving without muscle loss.

📈 Phase 5–6 (Weeks 17–26)

Training days stay at 2,300. Add a refeed day once per week (2,700 kcal, mostly carbs) to reset leptin and keep metabolism from crashing. Advanced technique — you've earned it by now.

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES
✅ 180g PROTEIN DAILY — NO EXCEPTIONS — This single number matters more than anything else. Hit it every day including rest days. It's what keeps your muscle while the fat leaves.
✅ 8,000–10,000 STEPS EVERY DAY — You start sedentary. Walking burns 250–350 extra kcal/day without affecting recovery. Over 6 months that's ~5 lbs of fat from walking alone.
✅ DRINK 3.5L WATER DAILY — Dehydration tanks performance, makes you feel falsely hungry, and slows fat metabolism.
✅ SLEEP 8 HOURS MINIMUM — Growth hormone releases during deep sleep. Poor sleep spikes cortisol, which stores fat and burns muscle.
❌ NEVER GO BELOW 2,000 KCAL — Too big a deficit = muscle loss, metabolic adaptation, hormonal crash, and rebound.
❌ NO ALCOHOL IN PHASE 1 — Give yourself 4 clean weeks. Alcohol halts fat oxidation for 48 hrs and destroys sleep quality.
❌ DON'T SKIP PROTEIN ON REST DAYS — Muscle breakdown is 24/7. Rest days need just as much protein as training days.
